Who we are

TRAUMABELOWABYSS

SMALL TEAM.
BIG FEELING.

OUCH Studios is an independent team making games in Fortnite. We work across programming, art, animation, sound and level design, following an idea until it becomes a place you can step into.

From TRAUMA to ABYSS, every world has a different way of pulling you in. OUCH Studios is part of OAV Studios AB, based in Sweden.

How we work

  1. 01

    Feeling first

    Every world starts as a mood: a place, a sound, a reason to look over your shoulder. The mechanics come after.

  2. 02

    Better together

    Horror is best with someone next to you. We build for the scream you share.

  3. 03

    Small on purpose

    Four people and no layers between an idea and the map it becomes.
