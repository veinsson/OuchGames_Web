[ How We Build ]

01

Dread, not jumpscares

The scariest part of a horror map is almost never the monster. It’s the certainty, building slowly, that there is one.

02

Detail you feel

We’ll spend longer on how a room is lit than on what’s waiting inside it — because the lighting is the reason you walk slower.

03

Cinematic pacing

Most of us came out of film school. We storyboard first, and treat framing and rhythm as seriously as the scare itself.
