The premise

ONE MORE
DIVE.

You crew the Mule, a submarine falling apart faster than you can pay for it. Every three days the Repair Bill comes due, and the next one is always bigger.

  1. 01

    Dive

    Pick a station and take the Mule down. Keep it running and push its range.

  2. 02

    Salvage

    Bring back whatever you can carry, then make room for one more haul.

  3. 03

    Get out

    Different threats punish different habits. Learn them, or don’t come back.

  4. 04

    Pay the bill

    Every three days you dock and settle up. The next bill is always bigger.

Field guide

KNOW WHAT’S
DOWN THERE.

Every creature punishes one habit. Learn which one, and stop doing it.

  • Punishes · going off aloneLucyStay near your crew. She only hunts divers who are on their own.
  • Punishes · looking awayPoseWhile any living diver is looking at it, it can’t move and can’t hurt anyone.
  • Punishes · running in straight linesLurkerNever try to outrun it down a long corridor.
  • Punishes · moving while watchedLamp MawWatch the beam. It always shows where the Lamp Maw is looking.
