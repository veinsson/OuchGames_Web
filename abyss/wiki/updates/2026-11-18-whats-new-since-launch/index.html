    What’s new since launch

    The Halloween update brought a new map, the Hotel, a tugboat mission and a new creature, the Warden. Plus a collection to fill and upgrades to buy.

    VVelloCo-founder · Lead programmer

    It’s been a while since our last post, so here’s everything that has landed in ABYSS since launch.

    Halloween: the tugboat mission

    A stranded tugboat sits in the harbor, and its captain talks to the crew through an old CRT terminal. His engine died, and he needs three parts to get underway: a battery, a propeller and a bag of potato chips.

    • Talk to the captain at the terminal. It’s a fully voiced conversation with choices, and his face lip-syncs on the screen.
    • Find the parts on your dives, bring them back and drop them in the service hatch. He reacts to every part, and to junk.
    • Once the last part is in, the engine starts. Say goodbye, then stay on the dock and watch the tug sail out into the fog. Trust us.

    It’s a crew job. One set of parts fixes the tug for the whole crew, anyone can deliver any part, and progress is saved, so parts from earlier sessions count. Once your crew finishes it, the tug is gone for that crew for good.

    The tugboat is a Halloween exclusive. Everyone in the session gets the rewards, once per player:

    • Fogcutter flashlight, an event-exclusive skin with 20% more beam reach
    • 80 HEX
    • 75 XP

    A new map: the Hotel

    The Halloween update also opened a new station to dive: the Hotel. We’ll share more about it soon, and it’s on the dive sites page already.

    A new creature: the Warden

    Something new walks the halls, with a pumpkin for a head, a single burning eye and a shotgun in its hands. About three shots and you’re done, but it can miss. Keep moving, zigzag, and never stand still where it can see you. Its creature file has the rest.

    Your collection

    Every valuable you find now goes into your own collection, which you can open from anywhere. It shows how many of the valuables you’ve found, ranked from Common through Uncommon, Rare and Legendary to Abyssal, with the ones you’re still missing greyed out.

    The collection is yours alone, not shared with your crew, and it’s saved for good. You get a notification when you pick up something that isn’t in it yet, and a reward when you reach a collection goal, like finding five Common valuables.

    Upgrades

    You can now spend HEX on upgrades that make your gear better: longer scans, more scan reach, more battery capacity, longer stims and more.

    Also

    • The terminal map buttons have been reworked.

    See you down there.

    — Vello
