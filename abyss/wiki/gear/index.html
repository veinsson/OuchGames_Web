    Equipment

    Gear & shop

    What you can buy with CR before a dive, what you wear on your head, and what is in the Depot. Every tool takes a slot you could have filled with loot, so pack light.

    The terminal shop

    The SHOP tab on the MULE terminal sells tools for CR. Orders land at the hatch of the sub you’re standing in a second or two later.

    IconItemPriceRarityWhat it does
    Low-tier flashlight35 CRToolA basic light with limited brightness and battery.
    Flashlight85 CRCommonA reliable light with more brightness and battery.
    Door Key8 CRCommonUnlocks one secured door and is used up.
    Blowtorch120 CRToolOpens locked doors. Hold use to burn; 45 s of fuel.

    • The stock rolls again every round. Each row holds a few units at most, and some rows sell out.
    • There is always a flashlight row, and there is always enough on the shelves to work with.
    • A Restock Token (50 V-Bucks) refills the shop straight away.
    • Anything you buy can be sold again at the sell press, for less.

    Flashlights

    Your flashlight is your most important tool. The battery drains while the light is on, so turn it off when you don’t need it. Shake the flashlight to recharge it.

    Light works both ways. GRIN can see your beam, and a Lamp Maw’s own beam shows you where it is looking.

    85 CR · Terminal shop

    Flashlight

    A reliable light with greater brightness and battery capacity. The standard torch, and the only one that can wear a skin.

    35 CR · Terminal shop

    Low-tier flashlight

    A basic light with limited brightness and battery capacity. A dimmer beam, and it can’t wear skins.

    The shotgun

    Shooting isn’t always the answer. A shot sends a Snag into a fury, makes Lucy fight back and sets off a Lamp Maw straight away. Two contracts pull in opposite directions: one wants ten shots fired, another wants a whole expedition without a single one.

    Helmets

    You wear one helmet at a time, and picking up a new one replaces the old one. The Standard helmet comes free with your scuba tank. The others are permanent V-Bucks unlocks in the Depot.

    Free

    Standard

    Comes with the scuba tank. No perk.

    450 V-Bucks · Hands-free light

    Searchlight

    A powerful lamp on the crown, leaving both hands free for salvage. Great for finding valuables in an unpowered wreck, and for showing whatever lives there exactly where you are.

    600 V-Bucks · See without being seen

    Nightvision

    Picks out salvage in near-total darkness without casting a beam. Slower to read than a lamp, but it doesn’t announce you.

    500 V-Bucks · Haul more, slow less

    Lightweight

    Your cargo counts as about three quarters of its weight, so a heavy haul slows you down less.

    800 V-Bucks · Take a harder hit

    Reinforced

    Layered plate over the pressure seal for more health. The only reason some divers come back up at all.

    800 V-Bucks · Harder to notice

    Signature dampening

    Muffles your sound and silhouette, so creatures have to be much closer to notice you. When the crew is together, they tend to notice somebody else first.

    The Searchlight and Nightvision run on their own helmet battery, separate from your flashlight. It recharges while switched off.

    Colourways (green, yellow, blue and red) cost 30 HEX each and are cosmetic. The Pumpkin helmet is a Halloween reward.

    Flashlight skins

    A skin restyles any standard flashlight you pick up; you still need to find or buy the flashlight itself. Equip skins in the Codex, and your equipped skin is saved between sessions. Buy a skin while you have no flashlight and you get a free one wearing it.
