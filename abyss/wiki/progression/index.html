    Progression

    Your rank, HEX and cosmetics are yours for good. They survive a lost MULE, a reset run and a bad night. This is how you earn them.

    Earning XP

    You…XP
    Sell loot1 per 2 CR
    Survive a day and extract120
    Pay the Repair Bill500
    Survive a medium / high pressure dive+25 / +50
    Kill a creature25
    Make a repair (leaks and the like)10
    Crank the engine3

    Each level needs a bit more XP than the last: 120 for level 2, 210 for level 3, and 90 more for every level after that.

    Every level-up pays HEX, starting at 10 and growing by 2 per level, up to 50 HEX a level.

    Your run streak counts how many expeditions in a row your crew has made it home from.
