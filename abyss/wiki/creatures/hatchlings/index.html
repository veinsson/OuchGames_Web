    Hatchlings

    Small creatures released when a Biomass Egg hatches.

    Punishes
    Rough handling
    Found
    Inside stations
    Shows up
    From an egg
    Death screen
    IT HATCHED

    How to survive

    1. Carry Biomass Eggs gently. Sprinting with one or dropping it agitates it, and at full agitation it hatches.
    2. If it’s getting agitated, slow down. Walk it home.

    Watch for

    • A Biomass Egg in someone’s hands, carried too fast.
