    How they show up

    • A station only holds so many creatures. The limit grows with each day of the run, and the dangerous ones take up more of it.
    • They don’t all arrive at once. They trickle in during the dive, so a quiet station doesn’t stay quiet.
    • On day 1 a station might start empty.
    • Open water is calm most of the time. When something goes wrong out there, more of them come.
    • The training dive only has Snags inside and Scuttles outside.
    • Killing a creature is worth 25 XP.
