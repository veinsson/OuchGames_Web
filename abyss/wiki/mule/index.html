    MULE-7 · Salvage submarine

    The MULE

    Home, getaway car and money pit. The crew shares one submarine and all its upgrades, and every three days it costs more to keep it in one piece.

    Upgrades

    There are five upgrades, each from level 1 to 5. Whoever buys one pays from their own CR, but the level belongs to the whole crew. All upgrades are lost if the MULE implodes.

    What breaks on the way down

    At most one thing goes wrong per trip, and the deeper the destination, the more likely it is. The MULE won’t travel on until it’s fixed. Each repair is worth 10 XP.

    The most common · Gets worse with depth

    Hull leak

    One to three holes open early in the trip and water starts rising. Patch each hole at its button: stop the needle inside the target zone.

    • After 30 s of flooding the power cuts out. Someone has to hold the generator in the engine room for 5 s to bring it back.
    • After 55 s the hull breaches. The sub explodes, everyone aboard dies and the day is lost.
    • The HULL upgrade means fewer leaks and fewer holes.

    Fairly common

    Steam leak

    Up to five steam pipes burst and steam fills the sub. Close every red valve to clear it.

    Rare near the surface · More likely deep

    Something hits the sub

    A few seconds into the trip something slams into the hull, and a moment later it gives way into a leak.

    Every trip

    Engine integrity

    The engine bar drains while you travel, and faster once it’s failing. Crank the engine button to add 10% (3 XP a crank). At 0% the engine goes offline and the MULE stops until it’s restored.

    The Repair Bill

    Every three days the bill is due at the Repair Dock, and it grows faster every cycle. These are rough amounts; each one varies a little.

    Paying gives you a 10% bonus and 500 XP. If you can’t pay, the MULE implodes and the run resets, unless someone settles it with Salvage the Run in the Depot.

    A new crew’s first dive is a guided training dive with a bill of only 15–30 CR.
