    What you’ll find

    Early on, most of a haul is Common, with a few Uncommon pieces and the odd Rare one. Legendary loot is a lucky find and Abyssal loot doesn’t turn up at all.

    That changes as the run goes on. The odds improve with every day you survive and every metre you dive. Deep into a run, at deep sites, Rare and Legendary finds become regular and Abyssal items start to appear.

    Three sites stack better odds on top of that: the Cave, the Hospital and the Sunken Temple, with the Temple best of all. Their bonus starts at half strength and grows over the first three weeks of a run.

    Heavy loot

    Heavy items take both hands. While you carry one you can’t switch slots until you drop it, so you can’t reach your flashlight or anything else. Weight also slows you down in steps, Heavy or not. The Lightweight helmet and the MULE’s CARGO upgrade both make a heavy haul easier.

    Loot that fights back

    Some of the best-paying salvage is trying to kill you.

    • Medical WasteLeaks a scent trail while carried. Creatures that cross the trail come to investigate.
    • BoomboxStarts a 60 s song that lures creatures to it. Drop it and run to clear a corridor; holding it is a bad idea.
    • Pressure MineSprinting with it or dropping it agitates it. At full agitation it explodes (120 damage, 9 m radius). Walk it home.
    • Biomass EggAgitates like the Pressure Mine, but at full agitation it hatches: it hurts you (35) and lets a creature loose in the station. Scuttles attack whoever carries one.
    • Ouija BoardCursed: it hurts whoever carries it, more the longer they hold it. Its value is hidden in game.

    Selling

    Loot is sold at the sell press at the Repair Dock. Selling earns XP as well as CR (1 XP for every 2 CR).

    Don’t stand on the press when it comes down. Whoever is on it gets sold for scrap with everything else.
