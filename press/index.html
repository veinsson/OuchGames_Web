Descriptions

ABYSS · short

A co-op deep-sea salvage horror game in Fortnite for one to four players. Crew a failing submarine, loot abandoned stations on the seabed and pay the Repair Bill before something finds you.

ABYSS · long

ABYSS puts up to four players aboard the MULE, a salvage submarine falling apart faster than they can pay for it. Each day the crew picks a dive site, survives whatever breaks on the way down, and loots a derelict station before the pressure timer runs out. Every three days the Repair Bill comes due, and the next one is always bigger.

Down in the stations, sixteen creatures each punish a different habit: going off alone, looking away, making noise, running in a straight line. Learning which is which is how a crew survives. Fail to pay the bill, and the MULE implodes and the run starts over.

OUCH Studios

OUCH Studios is an independent game studio from Jönköping, Sweden, making horror worlds in Fortnite. Founded in 2025 by Key, QP and Vello, with Teki, the studio has released 16 worlds, played for more than 264 million minutes. ABYSS is its biggest release so far.

På svenska

OUCH Studios är en oberoende spelstudio från Jönköping som gör skräckvärldar i Fortnite. Studion grundades 2025 och har släppt 16 världar som har spelats i mer än 264 miljoner minuter.

ABYSS är ett skräckspel för en till fyra spelare som samarbetar som besättning på en sliten bärgningsubåt. De dyker ner till övergivna stationer på havsbotten, bärgar det de kan bära och måste betala reparationsräkningen innan något hittar dem. Spelet släpps gratis i Fortnite den 15 oktober 2026.
